# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Project Maya to be a place where anyone can report a bug, ask a question, or send a change without being
mocked, harassed or excluded. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience,
identity, hardware budget or language.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being specific and kind in reviews: say what is wrong, why, and what would fix it;
- assuming good faith, especially with non-native speakers and first-time contributors;
- measuring before claiming, and saying what you did not measure;
- accepting that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- insults, personal attacks, or dismissive "just read the docs" answers;
- harassment, sexualised language or attention, or threats;
- publishing someone's private information (address, employer, machine names, logs with personal paths);
- sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, discussions and pull requests, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@mw00](https://github.com/mw00), through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Consequences range from a warning, to removing comments, to a temporary or permanent ban from the
project's spaces, depending on severity and repetition.
