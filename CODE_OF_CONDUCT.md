# Code of Conduct

Project Maya is a place to work on running large models on your own hardware. Everyone is welcome to take part.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. People here have different hardware, experience and first languages.
- Keep discussion on the work: reports, measurements, code and ideas.
- Disagree with reasons and data, not with people.
- Accept that maintainers decide what is merged, and that "no" or "not now" is a normal answer.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks, in any form.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Spam, or repeated off-topic posting after being asked to stop.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules.

## Reporting

If something here worries you, report it privately through GitHub: use "Report content" on the comment, or contact the
maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/mw00. Reports are handled privately.
